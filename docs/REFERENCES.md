## Research limits

- This was a public-surface review, not an authenticated usability test or reverse engineering of any app.
- No internal AstroLive analytics, cohorts, conversion rates, retention rates, customer interviews, or experiment results were available.
- Absence of a feature from the opened pages means only **“not observed on these public surfaces,”** not that the feature is absent from every version of the product.
- Search snippets and pages that were not opened are not cited.
