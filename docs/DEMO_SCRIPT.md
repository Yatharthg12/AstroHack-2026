## Judge questions: concise answers

**Is the astrology generated by AI?**  
No. Pulse uses transparent fixed templates. The models estimate product behavior only.

**Are these real users or metrics?**  
No. The dashboard and model targets are generated synthetic demonstrations with seed 2026.

**What is structural about the referral?**  
The invite delivers mutual value only after an independent invitee action and dual consent; it records creation, open, and completion rather than stopping at a share button.

**What protects private context?**  
Separate consent records, a brief-level check-in-sharing choice, random expiring tokens, safe referral views, and cascade reset.

**What would be tested first?**  
Pulse eligibility against the current experience for Weekly Guided Users and D7 retention, with helpfulness, opt-out, and safety guardrails.

**Are the behavioral models production-ready?**  
No. On synthetic holdout data, churn PR-AUC is 0.6077 and intent PR-AUC is 0.3459. The churn operating point favors recall and creates many false positives. The evidence is transparent, but neither model is validated for production targeting.
