Consent map

What moves—and only when.

Pulse entries remain private unless a user selects the exact recent mood, confidence, and concern text shown in Bridge, saves that frozen draft, and separately approves it. Later Pulse entries are not added. An astrologer sees only the approved brief and snapshot. Circle receives no private notes.

1
Private by default

Profile → Pulse → Journey

2
Approved for consultation

Edited brief + frozen selected entries → Astrologer Console

3
Mutually consented

Broad shareable states → Circle Insight

Prototype limitation: Data is stored in server-side SQLite, not on your device. Signed-session access expires after 8 hours and has no login/recovery; if the cookie is lost, surviving rows cannot be retrieved or deleted through this UI. The supplied free Render `/tmp` database is ephemeral and may disappear on restart or deploy. This is not an astrology service, medical service, emergency resource, or live AstroLive transaction.
